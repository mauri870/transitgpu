# Injection-recovery of the searches

What the searches find in light curves with planets of known period and signal to noise, and how often they flag a light curve with nothing in it. The plan below is written before the evaluation is run; the results are added under Results when it is.

## Question

For which signal to noise does each search return the injected period, and at what rate does each flag a light curve with nothing injected? The searches: exact TLS on the GPU (`transitgpu.tls`, backend `gpu`, the reference), binned TLS (`gpu-binned`), the box search (`transitgpu.box_search`) and the hybrid (`transitgpu.bls_tls_hybrid`) with 1, 5, 10 and 20 refined peaks. The statistics differ between the searches, so a fixed SDE is not a comparison; each search is compared at the SDE that it exceeds in a given share of light curves with nothing injected.

## Method

Light curves. Synthetic (`generator.py`, all seeded): white noise of 1000 ppm; red noise (700 ppm white plus an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process of 500 ppm and a correlation time of 0.3 d, the errors given as the 700 ppm); a variable star (700 ppm white plus a rotation of 3000 ppm with a first harmonic at 40 percent, a period log-uniform from 0.8 to 10 d). Red, variable and real light curves are divided by the biweight filter of wotan 1.10 (`method="biweight"`, `window_length=3` days, `break_tolerance=0.5`) after the planet is injected, the same filter for every search; white noise is not filtered. wotan, batman and astropy are in the dependency group `validation` of the package with their versions, not in its dependencies. Real: the 12 quiet 2-minute TESS dwarfs of `stars.json`, three each of F, G, K and M by the effective temperature of the TIC (F 6000 to 7300 K, G 5300 to 6000 K, K 3900 to 5300 K, M below 3900 K) with log g at least 4.0; for each, the TIC, sector, effective temperature, file name, URL and SHA-256 (`fetch.py` downloads and verifies them). A star is in the list if it is not in the TOI table (snapshot of 2026-10-21), has CROWDSAP at least 0.99, Tmag from 8.5 to 12, at least 17,500 cadences with quality flag 0, and a 0.5-day-binned 5-to-95 percent range of at most twice the white-noise expectation; the first three of each type that pass, in a seeded order, are taken. The flux is the PDCSAP flux with quality flag 0, divided by its median and by the same filter; the errors given to every search are the median of the reported errors, since binned TLS needs equal errors.

Baselines. One sector (27.4 d at 2 minutes, 19,728 points) is the main case. Four sectors (1 d gaps; 78,912 points) is a second case with fewer light curves.

Planets. A circular planet around a star of the solar density (a / R = 4.2 P^(2/3), P in days), with the transit integrated over the cadence (batman). Periods 0.8, 1.6, 3.2, 6.4 and 12.8 d; target signal to noise 4, 6, 8, 10, 14 and 20, impact parameter uniform from 0 to 0.9; the epoch uniform over one period. The target S/N is S/N = sqrt(sum over the cadences of (1 - m_i)^2) / sigma_eff, with m_i the transit model at cadence i (for a flat transit, the depth times the square root of the number of in-transit points) and sigma_eff the noise per point that a transit of its duration sees in the light curve itself: 1.4826 times the median absolute deviation of the means of the filtered flux, without the planet, over non-overlapping windows of one transit duration (T14), times the square root of the number of cadences in a window (`generator.effective_sigma`). For white noise sigma_eff is the standard deviation per point (1000 ppm); for the red, variable and real light curves it is larger, because the mean over a transit does not fall as the root of the number of points when the noise is correlated. This is the treatment of correlated noise of Pont, Zucker and Queloz (2006, MNRAS 373, 231, doi 10.1111/j.1365-2966.2006.11012.x), who bin over the duration of the transit. It differs in the estimator: they separate a white and a red component, S/N = depth / sqrt(sigma_w^2 / n + sigma_r^2 / N_transits), and estimate the red part from the excess of the binned scatter over the white expectation; sigma_eff takes the scatter of the window means directly, with a median absolute deviation, which does not separate the components and is little moved by outliers. The planet is injected before the filter and the S/N is computed from the filtered light curve without it. The radius ratio is solved twice for the target (`generator.with_signal_to_noise`), since T14 depends on it. The thirty cells of period and S/N have the same number of replicates. The tables are by this S/N.

Searches. Each is run on each light curve over 0.5 to 14 d: exact, binned, box, and the hybrid with 20 peaks (window 1 percent), whose first 1, 5 and 10 peaks are the hybrids of those counts because the peaks are nested. The run time of the hybrid at each count is measured separately on a subset (every light curve in the timing subset runs the hybrid four times).

Recovery. A peak is right when its period is within 1 percent of the planet's and its transit time within half a duration of a transit; harmonic when its period is within 1 percent of 1/3, 1/2, 2/3, 3/2, 2 or 3 times the planet's. The harmonics are counted apart and are not found. A search finds the planet when its best peak is right and its SDE is at or above the threshold; the hybrid with k peaks finds it when one of the first k peaks (the period and transit time of its TLS refinement, else the box peak's) is right and the SDE of the box search is at or above the threshold.

Matched false-alarm rate. For each search, noise class and baseline, light curves with nothing injected (generated by the same function with the seeds of the null range) give the null distribution of the SDE. The threshold at a false-alarm rate of 5 percent is the 95th percentile of the null SDE of that search and is the main point; that at 1 percent is the 99th percentile. Each threshold is reported with a bootstrap interval of 1,000 resamples of the nulls (2.5 and 97.5 percent), since 1 percent of 1,000 nulls is 10 light curves. Completeness is the share of injected light curves that the search finds at that threshold, with its Wilson 95 percent interval, by S/N (pooled over the periods) and by period (pooled over S/N). For the real stars the nulls are the stars' own light curves with their 1-day blocks shuffled within the sector (100 shuffles of each star, after the star itself), because 12 stars give 12 unshuffled nulls; this null assumes that the blocks are exchangeable, which is the question of another evaluation.

Reference check. On 60 light curves of the main case (20 of each synthetic class) exact TLS on the GPU is compared with TLS 2.0.1's own exact backend on the CPU (`fused`).

Seeds. A light curve's seed is `1_000_000 * class + index`, class 0 white, 1 red, 2 variable, 3 real; injected index 0 to 1,199 (cell = index mod 30, replicate = index // 30) and null index 100,000 and up. The range 9,000,000 to 9,999,999 belongs to another evaluation. A case is identified by `class:baseline:kind:index` and a case whose line is in the output file is skipped before anything is generated.

Size. Main case: 40 replicates a cell (1,200 injected light curves) and 1,000 null light curves for each of the three synthetic classes; for the real stars 3 replicates a cell for each star (1,080 injected) and 100 shuffled nulls a star besides the star itself (1,212). Four sectors: 8 replicates a cell (240) and 300 null light curves for each synthetic class. In all 6,600 + 2,280 + 1,620 = 10,500 light curves.

## Reading rules

Written before the run.

1. Reference. Exact TLS on the GPU is the reference for the others if it finds the same best period as TLS 2.0.1's exact backend in all 60 light curves and the SDE agrees to a relative 1e-6. Otherwise the reference is the CPU backend and the difference is reported.
2. Expectation (argued): at every S/N the completeness ranks exact at least the binned, the box and the hybrid, as the template matches the injected shape; the hybrid's recall rises with the number of peaks and its run time grows with it. The expectation is wrong where the paired interval of another search's difference from exact lies wholly above zero.
3. Two searches are compared on the same light curves, so a difference of completeness has the score interval of Tango for the difference of paired proportions (Tango 1998, Statistics in Medicine 17, 891, doi 10.1002/(SICI)1097-0258(19980430)17:8<891::AID-SIM780>3.0.CO;2-B; `analyse.tango`, whose coverage is tested), from the light curves that one finds and the other does not. The hybrid is reported as a search with a measured recall if, at 5 peaks, the lower bound of that interval for (its completeness minus that of exact) at the 5 percent false-alarm rate, over S/N 8 to 20 pooled over periods, is at least -0.10 in every noise class of the main case. Otherwise it is reported as candidate generation, with the recall it has at each peak count.
4. Binned TLS is reported with the completeness it has beside exact's, by class, with the same paired interval of the difference, and the periods at which that interval lies wholly below zero.
5. The tables carry the harmonics: for each search, the share of the injected light curves whose best peak is a harmonic, by period.

## Cost

Timed on one case on 2026-10-24, AMD Radeon RX 7900 XTX, warm calls of one process: one sector, 0.13 s exact, 0.06 s binned, 0.01 s box, 0.25 to 0.43 s hybrid with 20 peaks (0.02 to 0.04 s at 1 peak, 0.08 s at 5, 0.13 s at 10, 0.25 to 0.33 s at 20): 0.45 s a light curve; four sectors, 1 to 2 s exact, 0.18 s binned, 0.04 s box, 0.6 to 0.7 s hybrid: about 3 s. The first call in a process takes about 12 s (the kernels compile). The filter of red and variable light curves takes about 2 s a light curve at one sector and 3.3 s at four, on the CPU. GPU time: 10,500 light curves, of which the 1-sector 8,880 at 0.45 s (1.1 h) and the 4-sector 1,620 at 3 s (1.4 h): about 2.5 hours; the CPU time of the filters is 3 hours, shared by four worker processes beside the device. Wall-clock time about 3 hours in three parts: the main case of the synthetic classes (about 1 hour), the real stars with the reference check and the hybrid timings (about 0.5 hour), and four sectors (about 1.4 hours). Resumable; a STOP file ends a part between light curves.

## Results

First attempt, with the S/N against the injected white noise (1000 ppm for white, the 700 ppm white part for red and variable): completeness was near zero in the red and the variable classes at every S/N up to 20, for every search. For exact TLS at the 5 percent false-alarm rate, 19 of 1,200 injected red light curves were recovered at a nominal S/N up to 20 (3 of 1,200 for the box search), against 0.95 of the white-noise light curves at a nominal S/N of 8. The mechanism: a correlated component of 500 ppm with a correlation time of 0.3 d does not average over a 2.7 hour transit, so the noise on the mean depth of 8.5 transits is about 172 ppm against 506 ppm of depth, an S/N of about 3 against the nominal 20. A completeness against a white-noise S/N overstates what a search finds in correlated noise. The S/N was redefined as above after these rows were read, so the third reading rule below (S/N 8 to 20) applies to the S/N of the new definition; the rule's numbers were not changed. The nominal-S/N rows of the red and variable injected light curves are kept (`nominal-snr/`), and the null rows, which do not depend on the S/N, are the same.

## Output

`python run.py PART DIRECTORY` writes `DIRECTORY/PART.jsonl`, one JSON object per light curve, and `python analyse.py DIRECTORY` reads every `*.jsonl` there and writes `DIRECTORY/results.json`.

`BINNED_KWARGS='{...}' python rerun_binned.py PART DIRECTORY` runs binned TLS alone on the same light curves with those keyword arguments of `power()`, into `DIRECTORY/PART.binned-bands.jsonl`; `python analyse_binned.py DIRECTORY EVALUATION_DIRECTORY` joins it by id to the rows of `run.py` there and writes `DIRECTORY/results-bands.json`: the matched thresholds, the completeness and the paired difference of the rerun minus exact beside binned minus exact.

`python box_variable.py DIRECTORY EVALUATION_DIRECTORY` runs three box searches on the 200 injected variable-class light curves of signal to noise 8 and the first 200 variable-class light curves with nothing injected: the box search of transitgpu, its SDE after the running-median detrending that TLS applies to its spectrum, and astropy's `BoxLeastSquares` with the periods of the box search and, at each period, the durations the box search scores there (the `snr` objective squared). One line a case goes into `DIRECTORY/box-variable.jsonl`; the counts of each search at the threshold of its own 200 nulls (with the Wilson interval, the right peaks and the harmonics whatever the SDE), the same for exact mode read from `EVALUATION_DIRECTORY/main-variable.jsonl` on the same cases, and the paired Tango differences (each search minus exact, astropy minus the box search, the detrended box minus the box search) go into `--out` (`paper/box_variable.json` by default). The question it answers: whether the loss of the box search in the variable class is the box statistic on this noise or this implementation of it.

`python roc.py DIRECTORY [--bands BANDS_DIRECTORY] [--real REAL_DIRECTORY] [--out ../paper/roc.json]` reads the per-case records of the parts main-white, main-red and main-variable (and `real.jsonl` of the real stars) and writes, for exact, binned, box and the hybrid with 5 peaks, the share of the injected light curves of signal to noise 8 and above that each finds against the false-alarm rate of the nulls, on a grid of rates from 0.001 to 1, with Wilson intervals, the order of the searches at 0.01 and 0.05 and the rates between which two curves cross. `python ../paper/scripts/roc.py` draws `paper/roc.pdf` from it.

`python null_sde.py DIRECTORY [--bands BANDS_DIRECTORY] [--real REAL_DIRECTORY] [--calibration ROWS.jsonl] [--out ../paper/null_sde.json]` writes the binned counts of the SDE of each search over the null light curves of each part, with the 5 percent threshold, and the distribution of the box search's 48-shuffle threshold of the noise-only light curves of the calibration rows of `threshold.py`; `python ../paper/scripts/null_sde.py` draws `paper/null_sde.pdf` from it.

A record: `id` (`class:baseline:kind:index`, kind `inj` or `null`), `points`, `filtered_ppm` (standard deviation of the filtered flux, ppm), `snr` (the nominal target S/N, injected records; `star` for a real star), `planet` (injected records: `period`, `impact`, `radius_ratio`, `epoch_fraction`, `first_transit`, `duration_days`), and one object for each of `exact`, `binned` and `box` with `period`, `t0`, `sde`, `seconds` and, for an injected record, `label` (`right`, `harmonic` or `wrong`). `hybrid` has `sde` (the box search's SDE), `seconds` (the call with 20 peaks) and `peaks`, a list in rank order of `rank`, `box_period`, `box_sde`, `period` and `t0` (the TLS refinement, null when it failed) and, for an injected record, `label`; the hybrid at 1, 5 and 10 peaks is the first 1, 5 or 10 of the list. `hybrid_seconds` (every eighth injected record) maps `"1"`, `"5"`, `"10"` and `"20"` to the seconds of that hybrid. `cpu` (the first 20 injected light curves of each synthetic class on one sector) has `period`, `t0`, `sde` and `seconds` of TLS 2.0.1's exact backend. `case_seconds` is the whole case. The number of transits is the baseline over the period and is not stored. The false-alarm threshold is not in a record: it is a number of a search, a class and a baseline, in `results.json`.

`results.json` has, for each part: `injected` and `null` (counts); `searches`, by `exact`, `binned`, `box`, `hybrid1`, `hybrid5`, `hybrid10` and `hybrid20`, each with, for the false-alarm rates `"0.05"` and `"0.01"`, `threshold`, `threshold_bootstrap` (the 2.5 and 97.5 percent), `by_snr` and `by_period` (for each value `found`, `n` and `wilson`, the Wilson 95 percent interval), and `harmonic_share_by_period` (`harmonic`, `n`, `wilson`) and `seconds_median`; `paired` for each rate, with `binned_minus_exact`, `box_minus_exact` and `hybridK_minus_exact`: `estimate`, `low`, `high` (Tango), `only_first`, `only_second`, `n`, for the injected light curves of S/N 8 or more; `hybrid_seconds` (for each peak count "1", "5", "10" and "20": `peaks`, `median`, `quartiles` [first, third] and `n`); `reference_check` (`n`, `same_period`, `largest_relative_sde_difference`).
